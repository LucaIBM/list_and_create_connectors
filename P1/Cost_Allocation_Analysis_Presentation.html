IT Resource Towers: The Central Hub

Receives From:

  • Cost Source (direct)
  • Fixed Asset Ledger
  • Labor
  • Time Tracking
  • Other Labor Allocations
  • Projects
  • Vendors
  • Other Cost Pools

Distributes To:

  • Business Services (Direct)
  • Business Services Refacturacion
  • Business Services before Splitting
  • Servers
  • Network Devices
  • End User Devices
  • Sink (non-billable)

Key Role: Aggregates all IT costs and distributes them based on charging type, IT perimeter, and billability rules.

Allocation Cardinality Types

Type Description Example
One-to-One Direct 1:1 mapping between source and target Cost Source → Vendors
One-to-Many One source distributes to multiple targets Labor → IT Resource Towers
Many-to-One Multiple sources consolidate to one target Business Services → Service Allocations Direct
Many-to-Many Complex relationships with filtering Applications → Business Services Splitting
Unknown Undefined relationships (typically Sink) Service Allocations Indirect → Business Unit Allocation

Cost Distribution Methods

Evenly

Equal distribution across all targets

Use Cases:
  • Direct charging
  • Simple 1:1 mappings
  • Default allocation

Weighted

Distribution based on specific metrics

Weighting Factors:
  • Quantity: Servers, Network, Business Services
  • Headcount: External Labor
  • Hours: Time Tracking
  • Custom Formulas: Using SOURCE and field references
  • Weighting Factors: Projects, ITRT

Key Filtering Logic

Charging Type

  • Servicio: Service-based charging
  • Refacturación: Re-billing scenarios
  • Overhead: Indirect costs

Organizational

  • Is Corp: CORP vs NO CORP
  • Is IT Perimeter: Within IT scope
  • Business Unit ID: Organizational units

Billability

  • Is Billable: Yes/No
  • Is Direct Charging: Direct vs allocated

Technical

  • Allocation Source: Tracks cost origin
  • Plan Type: Actuals vs other
  • Unit of Measure: Ensures compatibility

Geographic

  • Country: Geographic allocation
  • Country to Recharge: Billing country

Special Allocation Scenarios

1. Overhead Allocation

Known Unit of Measure: Distributed proportionally to Business Services with matching UoM

Unknown Unit of Measure: Distributed to services with matching Unit and Service only

2. Refacturacion (Re-billing)

Non-IT for IT: Goes directly to Business Services

IT for IT: Routes through Business Services before Splitting or directly to Servers

3. Sink Allocations (Non-billable)

  • NO CORP: Costs outside corporate structure
  • No IT Perimeter: Costs outside IT scope
  • Not Billable: Explicitly marked as non-billable

Potential Considerations

1. Circular Dependencies

Issue: Business Services before Splitting → Servers → Business Services creates a loop

Impact: May require careful sequencing or iteration logic to converge

2. IT for IT Complexity

Issue: 7 different allocation strategies with hierarchical fallback

Impact: Requires thorough testing and documentation for maintainability

3. Data Quality Requirements

Issue: Extensive filtering requires accurate metadata in all fields

Impact: Missing or incorrect values could break allocation chains

Performance Implications

Complexity Factors

  • Many-to-many relationships with complex filters
  • Custom formula calculations requiring field references
  • Multiple allocation passes through same objects
  • Nested WHERE clauses with multiple conditions

Optimization Opportunities

  • Index key matching fields
  • Cache intermediate calculations
  • Optimize filter evaluation order
  • Consider parallel processing for independent paths

Recommendation: Monitor execution time for each allocation strategy and optimize the slowest ones first.

Audit Trail & Traceability

Tracking Mechanisms

  • Allocation Source field: Tracks origin of costs through the flow
  • Driver field: Identifies the allocation strategy used
  • User Description: Human-readable allocation explanation
  • Allocation Description: Detailed technical description

Challenges

  • Multi-hop allocations: Costs pass through 5-8 objects before reaching final destination
  • Complex transformations: Weighted distributions and custom formulas make tracing difficult
  • IT for IT loops: Costs may cycle through multiple IT for IT strategies

Best Practices

  • Maintain detailed logs of each allocation step
  • Implement cost lineage tracking
  • Regular reconciliation between source and destination totals

Recommendations for Usage

  1. Maintain Master Data Quality

    The system relies heavily on accurate metadata. Implement data validation rules and regular quality checks.

  2. Monitor Sink Allocations

    Track non-billable costs flowing to Sink. These represent optimization opportunities.

  3. Document IT for IT Rules

    The hierarchical fallback logic should be well understood by all stakeholders. Create detailed documentation.

  4. Validate Circular Flows

    Ensure Business Services ↔ Servers loop converges properly. Implement iteration limits and convergence checks.

  5. Test Edge Cases

    Complex filtering may create unexpected allocation paths. Comprehensive testing is essential.
